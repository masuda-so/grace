# Grace Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

Grace is provided by Ether LLC. This policy explains how the app handles data.

## Data stored by the app

Grace stores the content you create, such as text, dates, and selected images, in
the app’s local SwiftData store on your device. The app does not include an
analytics SDK, advertising SDK, account system, or remote content database.

When Grace offers a photo picker, the system picker gives the app only the item
you select. Grace does not request unrestricted access to your photo library.

## On-device assistant

On supported Apple devices and languages, assistant requests use Apple Foundation
Models on device. Grace does not send prompts or generated responses to a remote
AI provider or to Ether LLC. Model availability and system-level diagnostics are
controlled by the operating system and Apple’s applicable terms.

## Purchases

Apple processes purchases. Grace requests localized product information and reads
cryptographically verified StoreKit transactions to decide whether Pro access is
active. Ether LLC does not receive or store your payment-card details in the app.

## Retention and deletion

Local content remains on the device until you delete it in Grace, remove the app,
or erase the device. Deleting an item removes it from the app’s local store.
StoreKit transaction history is retained by Apple under Apple’s policies.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use
[Grace Support](https://ether-llc.com/apps/grace/support/). Do not include private
journal content, payment information, or other sensitive data in a support request.

This policy may change when Grace adds a new data practice. The effective date
above will be updated when that happens.
